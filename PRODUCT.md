# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, Canvas Confetti

## Users

Primary student: Sanskriti, preparing for the MAH-MBA/MMS-CET 2027 entrance exam in India (Asia/Kolkata timezone). Her target is top-tier Maharashtra business schools (e.g., JBIMS, SIMSREE). Her mentor (Prof. V. Sharma) provides oversight, validates AI diagnoses, and assigns targeted practice blocks.

## Product Purpose

Her Study Space is an adaptive, calm, and private study sanctuary that plans, teaches, tests, analyzes, remembers, and adapts. It replaces high-anxiety coaching chaos with one focused session at a time, learning directly from Sanskriti's recorded attempt history.

## Positioning

Unlike generic mock test portals or third-party quiz websites that dump random question links and punitive streak counters, Her Study Space runs an integrated in-app cognitive loop: every MCQ attempt is classified, repeated misconceptions trigger active AI hypotheses, grounded citations are pinned to real uploaded textbook pages, and parallel variants are generated on demand to calibrate weak concepts.

## Operating Context

Daily study blocks (target 3.5 hours) typically in evening hours. The CET exam comprises 200 questions in 150 minutes across 4 sections (Logical Reasoning, Abstract Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension) with zero negative marking. Exam date for 2027 is officially unannounced; the 24-week roadmap (28 Oct 2026 – 13 Apr 2027) serves as an internal preparation benchmark.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 100% in-app MCQ practice (Quick, Topic, AI Adaptive, Mistake Redo, Document-grounded, Sectional, and Full Simulation). No external redirects.
- Persistent Bayesian/frequency-weighted learner model updating topic mastery probabilities after every attempt.
- Dynamic error taxonomy: distinguishes one-off calculation slips from systematic base-value inversions and directional orientation errors.
- Document library with page-level text extraction and citation grounding.
- Full transparency: separate storage of verifiable Observed Facts vs editable AI Hypotheses.
- Dual-mode Socratic explanations in clear English or intuitive Hinglish.
- Dual roles: Student (Sanskriti) and Authorized Mentor (Prof. V. Sharma).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Her Study Space
- Tagline: One focused session at a time.
- Tone: Scholarly, serene, empowering, dignified. No toxic streak counters, no shame mechanics, no generic AI slop.

## Evidence on Hand

- Uploaded textbooks: *MAH-CET-Speed-Math-Formulas.pdf* (58 pages), *Logical-Puzzles-Advanced-Handbook.docx* (36 pages), *CET-2025-Actual-Paper-Analysis.pdf* (84 pages).
- 14 recorded baseline attempt sessions establishing current mastery profiles.

## Product Principles

1. Grounded Truth: Never cite a page that doesn't exist. Distinguish what the student actually clicked from what the AI hypothesizes.
2. Calm Rigor: Build quiet, focused momentum. Challenge Sanskriti at her growth edge without overwhelming her.
3. In-App Completeness: Practice, timers, scoring, explanations, and reviews happen within her sanctuary.
4. Transparent Memory: The student owns her learning profile; she can inspect, edit, or reset any hypothesis at will.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA compliant contrast (≥4.5:1), keyboard-navigable question palette, explicit focus rings, responsive mobile/desktop layouts.
